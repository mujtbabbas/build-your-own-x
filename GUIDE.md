# Reader's Guide — build-your-own-x

## What this project is

**Build Your Own X** is a collection of **step-by-step tutorials that teach
you how a technology works by having you rebuild it from scratch** — inspired
by Richard Feynman's "What I cannot create, I do not understand." Instead of
reading about a database, a Git, or a Docker, you follow a guided build of one
and come away actually understanding it.

## How the content is organised

Everything lives in [`README.md`](README.md), in a single **Tutorials**
section with one "Build your own …" group per technology — 30 groups,
including:

Build your own `3D Renderer` · `AI Model` · `Augmented Reality` ·
`BitTorrent Client` · `Blockchain / Cryptocurrency` · `Bot` ·
`Command-Line Tool` · `Database` · `Distributed Systems` · `Docker` ·
`Emulator / Virtual Machine` · `Front-end Framework / Library` · `Game` ·
`Git` · `Memory Allocator` · `Network Stack` · `Neural Network` ·
`Operating System` · `Physics Engine` · `Processor` · `Programming Language` ·
`Regex Engine` · `Search Engine` · `Shell` · `Template Engine` ·
`Text Editor` · `Visual Recognition System` · `Voxel Engine` ·
`Web Browser` · `Web Server`.

Inside each group, every entry is a tutorial link **labelled with the language
it is written in** (for example, a Kafka-like distributed system in Java, or
an introduction to ray tracing for a 3D renderer in C++), so you can pick the
same technology in a language you know — or one you want to learn.

Supporting files: `ISSUE_TEMPLATE.md` and the README's **Contribute** section
for proposing tutorials, and **Origins & License** for the project's roots
(content is CC0).

## How to use it

1. Pick the technology you want to understand from the **Tutorials** list.
2. Choose a tutorial in your preferred language.
3. Follow it end to end and build the thing — reading alone is not the point
   of this list.
